# ✨ Seeing Your Mastered Kanji

In Kanji Mastery, you can now see exactly which kanji you've studied, organized into three sections:

- 🟢 **Mastered** (green) - Kanji you've fully learned
- 🟡 **In Review** (amber) - Kanji in your review rotation
- 🔵 **In Learning** (blue) - Kanji you're currently learning

## ⏰ Why Mastered Takes Time

You might notice kanji don't appear in "Mastered" right away - this is intentional! 🎯

Our spaced repetition system requires kanji to reach a **21+ day review interval** with **90% accuracy** before marking them as mastered.

This ensures you truly know them long-term, not just short-term memorization. 🧠💪

New kanji start in "Learning", graduate to "Review" as you prove retention, and finally reach "Mastered" after consistent success over weeks.

Even better - each kanji is now **clickable**! 👆 Tap any kanji in these grids to open the full details modal with all the readings, meanings, and example sentences.

## 📝 Saving Examples to Your Lists

For the extensive examples you see when learning a kanji - you can save any of them to your personal lists! Here's how:

1. 👀 When viewing kanji details or examples, look for the **"Add to List"** button (list icon)
2. 🎨 Choose an existing list or create a new one with a custom name, emoji, and color
3. 📚 Your saved items are available anytime at **Lists** in the main menu

## 🎮 Using Your Lists for Study

Your saved lists aren't just for reference - they're fully integrated study tools:

- 📖 **Browse Mode** - Review your saved items with audio pronunciation
- 🃏 **Flashcard Mode** - Study with flip cards showing content, readings, and meanings
- 🏆 **Drill/Review Mode** - Practice with SRS-powered multiple choice quizzes that track your progress

Happy studying! 🌸
