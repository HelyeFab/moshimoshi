# Kanji Browser Study: Marketing Positioning

**Status:** Draft  
**Date:** 2026-03-25

## Core Framing

Kanji Browser browsing is free.

Kanji Browser study is not framed as “pay to click study.”
It is framed as:

- **free users can unlock and study up to 10 kanji**
- **those unlocked kanji remain available to re-study**
- **premium users can unlock unlimited kanji**

This is the most learner-friendly monetization story for the feature.

## Why This Message Works

It communicates:

- generosity
- permanence
- fairness
- a clear reason to upgrade

It avoids the bad feeling of:

- “I lost a session because I clicked the wrong thing”
- “I’m being charged every time I retry”

## Recommended Copy

### Short UI copy

- **Unlock up to 10 kanji for study on the free plan**
- **Re-study unlocked kanji anytime**
- **Premium unlocks unlimited kanji study**

### Upgrade copy

- **You’ve unlocked all 10 free kanji study slots**
- **Upgrade to Premium to unlock unlimited Kanji Browser study**

### Helper copy

- **Browsing is always free**
- **Only new kanji unlocks count toward the free limit**

## Product Story

The Kanji Browser is designed to let everyone explore kanji.

The paid distinction is not whether users can see the content.
It is whether they can build an ever-growing personalized study path through it.

Free plan:

- browse freely
- unlock a starter set of kanji for study
- return to those unlocked kanji as often as needed

Premium plan:

- everything above
- unlimited kanji unlocks
- no ceiling on long-term study growth

## Best Value Proposition

This is the strongest short description:

> Browse every kanji for free. Unlock and study your first 10 for free. Upgrade to Premium to study unlimited kanji.

## Messaging Notes

Prefer:

- **unlock**
- **study slots**
- **unlimited kanji study**

Avoid:

- **session limit**
- **daily study attempts**
- **paywall**
- **practice credits**

This feature should feel like:

- a growing study library

not:

- an arcade meter

## Strategic Benefit

This model supports both:

- learner trust
- premium conversion

Because the free offer is:

- concrete
- durable
- meaningful

And the premium benefit is:

- obvious
- permanent
- easy to explain
