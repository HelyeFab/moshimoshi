# Stage C: Segmentation

This stage begins only after Stage A and Stage B are accepted.

Goal:
- turn transcript text into proper repeat-worthy units similar in quality to Miraa

Requirements:
- text quality must be good enough to segment meaningfully
- audio and text must correspond
- segmentation policy may differ by content kind, especially lyrics vs speech

Evaluation dimensions:
- transcript text correctness
- segment boundary correctness
- segment-to-audio correspondence

Not assumed:
- one universal segmentation rule for all content

Likely policy split:
- speech / dialogue
- lyrics / lineation

Primary question:
- once playback is trustworthy, what is the right repeat unit for each content type?
