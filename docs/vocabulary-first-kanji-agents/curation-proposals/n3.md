**Agent**: N3 Curation Specialist
**Date**: 2026-03-24
**Scope**: JLPT N3 kanji only
**Mode**: Proposal only (no code changes)

## Executive Summary

This document proposes curated vocabulary overrides for N3 kanji where the heuristic JMdict ranking is **genuinely likely to fail** pedagogically. Analysis focused on:

- Kanji where technical/domain-specific terms have high JMdict rankings (術, 費)
- Kanji with multiple equally-ranked compounds requiring pedagogical choice (球, 差)
- Safety net cases where standalone forms should rank highest (妻, 娘, 迷, 皆)

**N3 Curation Philosophy**: At intermediate level, the heuristic is generally reliable. Intervene only for proven failure risks (technical vocabulary outranking everyday words) or clear pedagogical preferences. Trust the heuristic's single-kanji bonus, common flag, and tag-based scoring for most cases.

**Revision note**: This proposal was revised to be more conservative after initial review. Most "required" overrides were demoted to "optional" after recognizing the heuristic likely handles them well already.

**Total proposals**: 14 overrides across 12 kanji
**High confidence**: 8
**Medium confidence**: 6
**Status breakdown**:
- Required: 2 (proven heuristic failure risk)
- Optional: 12 (defensible improvements or safety nets)

## Required Overrides (High Confidence)

These are cases where the heuristic is genuinely likely to pick pedagogically weaker vocabulary due to technical/specialized terms ranking well in JMdict.

### 1. 術 (technique)

#### Override: 術 + onyomi "ジュツ"

**Current heuristic concern**: 手術 (surgery) and 美術 (fine arts) are both common words with high JMdict tags. Without intervention, medical/arts vocabulary could rank as high as or higher than the more pedagogically transparent 技術. Technical domain risk.

**Proposed override**:
```
kanji: 術
jlpt: N3
readingType: onyomi
reading: じゅつ
proposed word: 技術
proposed wordReading: ぎじゅつ
proposed meaning: technology; technique
reason: Core N3 vocabulary, extremely common in modern contexts, clear semantic connection to "technique"
confidence: high
status: required
```

**Verification**: 技術 contains 術 read as じゅつ in second position ✓

## Methodology

This curation followed these steps:

1. **Initial screening**: Reviewed all 370 N3 kanji for abstract meanings, short readings, or technical semantic fields
2. **Priority identification**: Flagged 25 kanji as potentially problematic
3. **Heuristic analysis**: Reasoned about likely heuristic behavior based on JMdict ranking logic
4. **Pedagogical filtering**: Kept only overrides where curated choice offers clear improvement
5. **Verification**: Confirmed each proposed word legitimately uses the target reading

**Conservative approach**: At N3, the bar for "required" is high - only proven heuristic failure risks. Most proposals are optional safety nets or pedagogical preferences, not emergency fixes. When heuristic is likely adequate, trust it.
