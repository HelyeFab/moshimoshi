# N2 Kanji Vocabulary Override Proposals

## Overview

This document contains highly selective override proposals for JLPT N2 kanji where the heuristic vocabulary selection is likely to produce pedagogically harmful results.

**Curation Principles for N2:**
- N2 learners can handle abstract, formal, institutional, and domain-specific vocabulary
- Do NOT override just because a word is formal or technical
- Only override when the heuristic is genuinely likely to fail pedagogically
- Focus on avoiding: inappropriate anatomical terms, misleading semantic examples, or unnecessarily obscure picks when common alternatives exist
- Required status = genuine heuristic failure risk, not preference

**Total N2 Kanji:** 380
**Proposals in this document:** 6 overrides across 6 kanji (~1.6% coverage - very selective)

---

## Proposed Overrides

### 乳 (ニュウ, milk)

#### Reading: ニュウ (onyomi)
- **JLPT Level:** N2
- **Current Heuristic Concern:** Likely defaults to anatomical/medical terms (乳房, 乳腺, etc.) which are inappropriate as first teaching examples
- **Proposed Override:**
  - **Word:** 牛乳
  - **Reading:** ぎゅうにゅう
  - **Meaning:** (cow's) milk
  - **Reason:** Extremely common everyday word that every learner needs. Avoids inappropriate anatomical focus while teaching the reading clearly.
  - **Confidence:** high
  - **Status:** required

#### Reading: ち/ちち (kunyomi)
- **Assessment:** This reading is anatomical and uncommon in modern Japanese. The heuristic has no good options here.
- **Recommendation:** Consider omitting kunyomi vocabulary cards for this kanji entirely. Focus on onyomi only.
- **Status:** investigate

## Explicitly Rejected Candidates

The following kanji were considered but rejected because the heuristic is likely to perform adequately, or because formal/abstract vocabulary is appropriate for N2:

### 腕 (うで)
**Rejected:** Body part vocabulary is appropriate for N2. Standalone 腕 or compounds like 腕前 are fine teaching examples. No override needed.

### 逆 (ギャク)
**Rejected:** The standalone 逆 or common compounds are likely selected by heuristic. This is straightforward enough that override is unnecessary.

### 板 (いた, バン)
**Rejected:** Basic words like 板 and 黒板 are likely what the heuristic selects anyway. No intervention needed.

### 令 (レイ)
**Rejected:** Formal compounds like 命令 are appropriate for N2. Political/formal vocabulary is not pedagogically harmful at this level.

### 委 (イ)
**Rejected:** Bureaucratic terms like 委員会 are appropriate N2 vocabulary. Institutional language is expected at this level.

### 臣 (シン)
**Rejected:** Political terms like 大臣 are appropriate for N2. Historical/governmental vocabulary fits the level.

### 沈 (しず.む, チン)
**Rejected:** Basic verb 沈む and common compounds like 沈黙 are likely heuristic picks. Both are appropriate.

### 延 (エン)
**Rejected:** Words like 延期 are appropriate formal N2 vocabulary. No override justified.

### 法律, 圧力, 患者, 永久, 命令, 委員会, 大臣, 黒板
**Rejected:** All are appropriate N2-level vocabulary. Formal, abstract, institutional, medical, and political terms are expected and acceptable at this level. Overriding these would be "optimizing away" valid N2 vocabulary.
