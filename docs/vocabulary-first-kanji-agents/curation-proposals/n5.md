## Status Definitions

- **required**: Current heuristic produces pedagogically weak results (technical terms, opaque meanings, wrong semantic field, or ambiguous substring matches). Override is necessary for good N5 teaching.
- **optional**: Heuristic might work, but this ensures a specific high-value word is used. Nice-to-have safeguard.

## Proposals

### 1. 子 (child) — kunyomi こ

**Kanji**: 子
**JLPT**: N5
**Reading type**: kunyomi
**Reading**: こ

**Current heuristic concern**: Single-mora reading "こ" creates substring matching ambiguity. Heuristic may incorrectly score compounds where "こ" comes from different kanji (小, 古, 故) or mismatch position in compound readings.

**Proposed word**: 子ども
**Proposed wordReading**: こども
**Proposed meaning**: child
**Reason**: Most common N5 word using this reading, semantically transparent, universally taught in first-week vocabulary, clearly shows the こ reading.
**Confidence**: high
**Status**: required

**Verification**: JMdict confirms 子ども (こども) common: true

## Rationale for Required vs Optional Status

### Required Status (8 proposals)

These address confirmed heuristic weaknesses:

1. **子 + こ**: Single-mora substring matching creates false matches
2. **生 + いきる**: Okurigana ambiguity with い.ける (opaque ikebana usage)
3. **生 + せい**: Abstract compounds (生産) likely outrank 学生 in raw JMdict frequency
4. **上 + うえ**: 8 reading variants require explicit positional vs. movement separation
5. **上 + あがる**: Movement verb must be separated from position/giving verbs
6. **上 + じょう**: Abstract formal compounds likely outrank 上手
7. **下 + した**: Multiple kunyomi require explicit positional separation
8. **中 + なか**: Onyomi compounds likely dominate, standalone form needs safeguarding

### Optional Status (3 proposals)

These are safeguards where heuristic might work but we want certainty:

1. **生 + うまれる**: Heuristic might pick correctly, but ensuring it appears is valuable for "life/birth" semantic coverage
2. **生 + せい (先生)**: Secondary example; 学生 is primary, this adds cultural/honorific dimension
3. **中 + ちゅう**: Heuristic might pick correctly, but 中学校 is pedagogically strongest school example
