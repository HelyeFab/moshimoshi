# N1 Kanji Vocabulary Override Proposals

## Overview

This document contains highly selective override proposals for JLPT N1 kanji where the heuristic vocabulary selection is likely to produce pedagogically harmful results.

**Curation Principles for N1:**
- N1 learners can handle technical, specialized, abstract, formal, literary, and domain-specific vocabulary
- Do NOT override just because a word is technical, formal, or specialized
- Only override when the heuristic is genuinely likely to fail catastrophically
- Focus on avoiding: inappropriate content, completely archaic/obsolete terms when modern alternatives exist, or severe semantic opacity that actively misleads
- Required status = genuine catastrophic heuristic failure, not preference or optimization

**Total N1 Kanji:** 1,136
**Proposals in this document:** 4 overrides across 4 kanji (~0.35% coverage - extremely selective)

## Proposed Overrides

### 乳 (ニュウ, milk)

**Note:** If this kanji appears in both N2 and N1 datasets, inherit the N2 override.

#### Reading: ニュウ (onyomi)
- **JLPT Level:** N1 (also appears in N2)
- **Current Heuristic Concern:** Likely defaults to anatomical/medical terms (乳房, 乳腺, etc.) which are inappropriate as first teaching examples
- **Proposed Override:**
  - **Word:** 牛乳
  - **Reading:** ぎゅうにゅう
  - **Meaning:** (cow's) milk
  - **Reason:** Extremely common everyday word. Avoids inappropriate anatomical focus while teaching the reading clearly.
  - **Confidence:** high
  - **Status:** required

### 硝 (ショウ, nitrate)

#### Reading: ショウ (onyomi)
- **JLPT Level:** N1
- **Current Heuristic Concern:** Highly technical chemistry term; may default to overly specialized chemistry/explosives vocabulary when a more recognizable compound exists
- **Proposed Override:**
  - **Word:** 硝子
  - **Reading:** ガラス
  - **Meaning:** glass
  - **Reason:** While this is an ateji/irregular reading, 硝子 is a widely recognized form that N1 learners may encounter. Only override if heuristic picks obscure chemistry terms. Note: Standard ガラス (katakana) is more common, so this override is low priority.
  - **Confidence:** low
  - **Status:** investigate
  - **Note:** This may not need an override - ateji status and irregular reading make it questionable as a teaching example. Consider omitting vocabulary cards if no good options exist.

## Explicitly Rejected Candidates

The following N1 kanji were considered but rejected because the heuristic is likely to perform adequately, or because technical/specialized vocabulary is appropriate for N1:

### 鋳 (チュウ, い.る - cast metal)
**Rejected:** Technical manufacturing vocabulary like 鋳造 is appropriate for N1. Specialized domain terminology is expected and acceptable at this level.

### 腺 (セン - gland)
**Rejected:** Medical/anatomical vocabulary like 腺 or 扁桃腺 is appropriate for N1. Advanced learners need medical terminology.

### 蚕 (サン, かいこ - silkworm)
**Rejected:** While specialized, 蚕 and related sericulture terms are culturally relevant vocabulary. N1 includes specialized cultural/historical terms.

### 租 (ソ - tariff)
**Rejected:** Economic/legal terms like 租税 are appropriate N1 vocabulary. Financial and bureaucratic language is expected at this level.

### 慄 (リツ - tremble/afraid)
**Rejected:** Literary/formal expressions like 戦慄 are appropriate for N1. Literary vocabulary is a core part of advanced proficiency.

### 傲 (ゴウ - arrogant)
**Rejected:** Formal character descriptors like 傲慢 are appropriate N1 vocabulary. Abstract character/emotional terms are expected.

### 蔽 (ヘイ - cover/conceal)
**Rejected:** Formal/literary compounds are appropriate for N1, even if rare in everyday speech.

### 棺 (カン - coffin)
**Rejected:** 棺 or 棺桶 are legitimate vocabulary even if somber. N1 includes all registers including funeral/death-related terms.

### 窒 (チツ - plug up/suffocate)
**Rejected:** Technical terms like 窒素 (nitrogen) or 窒息 (suffocation) are appropriate N1 vocabulary. Scientific terminology is expected.

### 臼 (キュウ, うす - mortar)
**Rejected:** Traditional/cultural vocabulary is appropriate for N1, even if rarely used in modern daily life.

### 蛍 (ケイ, ほたる - firefly)
**Rejected:** Cultural/natural vocabulary like 蛍 is appropriate. Literary and seasonal terms are core N1 content.

### 喩 (ユ - metaphor/illustrate)
**Rejected:** Abstract rhetorical terms like 比喩 are appropriate N1 vocabulary. Meta-linguistic terminology is expected at advanced levels.

### 詔 (ショウ, みことのり - imperial edict)
**Rejected:** Historical/classical terms are appropriate for N1. Learners at this level encounter classical and historical texts.

### 弔 (チョウ, とむら.う - mourn)
**Rejected:** Formal ceremonial vocabulary like 弔う or 弔辞 is appropriate N1 content. Ceremonial language is part of advanced proficiency.

### Technical/Specialized Domain Terms
**Rejected as a category:** N1 is expected to include medical (腺), chemical (硝), manufacturing (鋳), agricultural (蚕), economic (租), and other specialized domain vocabulary. Technical terminology is not pedagogically harmful at N1 - it's the point.

### Literary/Classical Terms
**Rejected as a category:** N1 includes literary expressions (慄), classical language (詔), and formal ceremonial terms (弔). This is appropriate level content.

### Cultural/Historical Terms
**Rejected as a category:** Traditional tools (臼), cultural practices (蚕), seasonal references (蛍), and historical concepts are core N1 vocabulary.

## Curation Philosophy for N1

**N1 is fundamentally different from N5-N2:**
- Technical vocabulary is the point, not a problem
- Literary and classical terms are expected content
- Domain specialization (medical, legal, scientific) is appropriate
- Cultural and historical depth is part of advanced proficiency
- Abstract and formal language is core curriculum

**Therefore:**
- The heuristic baseline is likely MORE appropriate for N1 than for beginner levels
- Most "difficult" or "specialized" vocabulary is pedagogically correct, not problematic
- Override only when the heuristic produces something genuinely harmful, not just challenging
- When uncertain, trust the heuristic - N1 learners can handle it
